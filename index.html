Your media stays on your device

Reels InstaEdits processes the photos, videos, audio, fonts and documents you choose on your device. The Android app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, developer-operated cloud processing or in-app purchases. It does not request Internet access or transmit your media, projects or device information to us. Any upfront app purchase is handled separately by Google Play.

Offline editing and AI tools

English, Hindi and Spanish speech recognition, photo cutout, subject detection, beat analysis, speech cleanup and music ducking run locally. Speech models are included in the app and unpacked locally when needed. Caption text, compact audio analysis, imported logos, creator kits and photo/video overlays are stored locally. Bilingual caption translations are entered by the user. Audio is not uploaded and microphone access is not requested.

Files and permissions

Android's system picker grants access only to files you select. A cloud file provider you choose may download those files under its own privacy practices. We do not request broad library or storage access, contacts, location, camera or microphone permissions. The media playback library includes the normal WAKE_LOCK permission; no runtime approval is needed for it. The app keeps the screen awake during processing and does not perform unattended background exports.

Storage, security and deletion

Imported media, editable projects, creator kits and recovery data use Android's app-private storage. Temporary processing files and rendered exports use local cache. Reverse processing creates a local video copy while retaining the original for restoration. Temporary photo-tool files are removed when the tool closes; Android may also clear cached files. We rely on Android's application sandbox and device security, and do not claim additional app-level encryption.

Deleting a saved project removes its project entry; media shared with other projects and recovery history may remain. To remove all private local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > Reels InstaEdits > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Automatic app backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Export portable projects before clearing storage or uninstalling. Because there is no app account or developer-side media storage, there is no online account to delete.

Exports and sharing

Saving creates a new gallery copy or writes to the destination you choose in Android's save dialog. Gallery media, PDFs and portable project backups remain outside app storage until you delete them using the relevant file or gallery app. Exported projects may contain source media, captions and creator assets. Sharing or opening a file in another app sends it only through the destination you choose; that app's privacy policy then applies.

Support messages and external services

Contact adityapatil206999@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. The contact button opens your email app; no message is sent automatically. If you send an email, your address, message and any attachments are handled by your email provider and our Gmail inbox so we can respond. Do not send private media unless you intend to include it. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you can email us to request its deletion.

Opening the online policy or a license/source link uses your browser. GitHub hosts the policy and may receive ordinary website connection information under GitHub's privacy statement. Android, Google Play, email services, browsers and chosen file providers operate independently under their own policies. Their service activity is separate from the app's offline media processing.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the Google Play disclosures before releasing that change. This policy applies to the Android version of Reels InstaEdits.
