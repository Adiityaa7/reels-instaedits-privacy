FilmAdoge — Windows privacy policy
Effective date: 10 October 2026
Publisher: Adiityaaa7. Contact: adityapatil206999@gmail.com

Video Editor & Maker -FilmAdoge processes photos, video, audio, text and captions locally on your Windows computer. The app does not contain advertising, analytics or account registration and does not upload your media or project content to the publisher.

Video tool setup downloads a specific FFmpeg release from GitHub and verifies its SHA-256 against a value pinned in the application. It refuses a mismatch before installation. These servers receive normal connection information such as your IP address and user agent. No photo, video, audio or project data is included in these requests. Automatic caption recognition and AI cutout run locally.

Creative workspaces include separate, unmodified PhotoCraft, VectorCraft, FilmCraft, LightCraft, PdfCraft, EffectCraft and DesignCraft desktop applications. They process documents locally and have their own settings, editable formats and local recovery or library storage. FilmAdoge bundles the tested executables and checks their pinned SHA-256 hashes before launching them; it does not download or automatically replace these workspace executables at runtime. A workspace's optional user-initiated services, such as FilmCraft speech-model downloads or PdfCraft's Help > Check for updates, contact their upstream hosts. Those hosts receive normal connection information. FilmAdoge does not start workspace automation servers or configure external providers on your behalf.

Sending media to a workspace creates a local exchange copy. FilmAdoge also saves a snapshot of the originating project when using Send current canvas/source. Native workspace projects open in their existing locations so linked assets keep working; use Save As inside that workspace to preserve a separate copy. Exchange copies, snapshots, native libraries and exported results persist so projects can reopen. Returning a result creates an image layer or timeline clip; its editable workspace project remains separate.

The app reads files you choose. Editable projects, exported media and collected project folders are saved where you select. Photo projects embed images and imported fonts. Video projects reference source files unless you collect their media. Share projects only with people you intend to receive that content.

Local application data includes imported-font caches, saved styles, brand preferences, collage/cutout assets, recovery copies and video render caches. They are not uploaded. The app clears temporary render/transcription files after completion or cancellation. Recovery files may remain after interruption; render caches can be cleared in video project settings. Persistent assets referenced by projects should be retained until those projects are no longer needed.

To remove your data, delete your projects and exports using File Explorer and clear the app's local data after closing it. Legacy internal storage uses the Letterloom folder in local application data; Microsoft Store installations may redirect this into package storage. Removing this data can remove recovery copies or linked assets. No publisher account exists to delete.

Microsoft Store purchases and Windows diagnostics are handled by Microsoft under its own policies and your Windows settings. Emailing support shares the email and any attachments you choose to send; do not send media unless needed for your request. Contact adityapatil206999@gmail.com for privacy questions.

This policy applies to the Windows FilmAdoge app. The Android Reels InstaEdits policy is published separately on the same site. Changes to this policy will be reflected in the effective date.
