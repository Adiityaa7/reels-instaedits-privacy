FilmAdoge — Windows privacy policy

Versions 3.0 and 3.1

Updated 11 October 2026. This section applies to FilmAdoge 3.0 and the 3.1 update when installed. The policy for Windows version 2 remains below.

Publisher: Adiityaaa7. Contact: adityapatil206999@gmail.com.

FilmAdoge edits photos, videos, audio, captions, drawings, PDFs, motion compositions and layouts on your Windows computer. The editor does not include advertising, publisher analytics or publisher account registration. It does not upload your media or project contents to the publisher.

Internet connections

The video engine and required runtime libraries are included with the installed application. FilmAdoge 3 does not download or install an executable video engine at runtime.

Auto captions require a speech model. When you choose Download speech model or Repair speech model, the app downloads data files from OpenAI's model repositories hosted by Hugging Face. The download may use Hugging Face's delivery servers. These services receive normal connection information such as your IP address and requested file. No project, audio, video or transcript is included in those download requests. Model files are checked against pinned SHA-256 checksums, and installed contents are checked before loading for recognition. Repair downloads and verifies a replacement before replacing the installed model. Speech recognition runs locally after the download.

Opening a source, license, support or privacy link opens a browser or email application. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Your files and local storage

The app reads the files you choose. Editable projects and exports are saved where you select. A FilmAdoge project contains the native documents of its editing workspaces and generated artwork. Imported video and audio generally remain linked to their original files. Keep those originals available. Anyone receiving a project may be able to read its embedded content, file paths and text.

Local working data includes recovery projects, extracted and generated artwork, editing preferences, preview copies and downloaded speech models. Preview copies are optional lower-resolution working files; final video exports use the original sources. Working files and models may remain after the editor closes, after cancellation or after a crash.

The Rust editor stores working assets under FilmAdoge\RustEditor, recoveries under FilmAdoge\Recovery and optional preview copies under FilmAdoge\PreviewCache in local application data. The integrated video engine's preferences and speech models use FilmCraft in roaming application data, unless a custom data directory is configured. Version 3.1 also stores workspace preferences and brush presets under FilmAdoge\WorkspaceSettings in local application data. Library metadata, ratings, presets and document information may be included in saved projects. Microsoft Store installations may redirect these locations to package storage.

You can remove projects and exports using File Explorer. Before removing application data, close the editor and preserve any projects, recovery copies and linked assets you need. Removing cached assets can affect projects that still reference them. No publisher account exists to delete.

When you use copy or paste, supported editing tools read or write the Windows clipboard locally. File dialogs, presets and document tools do not send your edits to a publisher server. Opening external help or update links is handled by your browser or Microsoft Store.

Microsoft and support

Microsoft Store services and Windows diagnostics are handled by Microsoft under its policies and your Windows settings. If you email support, the publisher receives your email and the attachments you choose to send. Send media only when you intend to share it for support.

Windows version 2

Video Editor & Maker -FilmAdoge processes photos, video, audio, text and captions locally on your Windows computer. The app does not contain advertising, analytics or account registration and does not upload your media or project content to the publisher.

Video tool setup downloads a specific FFmpeg release from GitHub and verifies its SHA-256 against a value pinned in the application. It refuses a mismatch before installation. These servers receive normal connection information such as your IP address and user agent. No photo, video, audio or project data is included in these requests. Automatic caption recognition and AI cutout run locally.

The app reads files you choose. Editable projects, exported media and collected project folders are saved where you select. Photo projects embed images and imported fonts. Video projects reference source files unless you collect their media. Share projects only with people you intend to receive that content.

Local application data includes imported-font caches, saved styles, brand preferences, collage/cutout assets, recovery copies and video render caches. They are not uploaded. The app clears temporary render/transcription files after completion or cancellation. Recovery files may remain after interruption; render caches can be cleared in video project settings. Persistent assets referenced by projects should be retained until those projects are no longer needed.

To remove your data, delete your projects and exports using File Explorer and clear the app's local data after closing it. Legacy internal storage uses the Letterloom folder in local application data; Microsoft Store installations may redirect this into package storage. Removing this data can remove recovery copies or linked assets. No publisher account exists to delete.

Microsoft Store purchases and Windows diagnostics are handled by Microsoft under its own policies and your Windows settings. Emailing support shares the email and any attachments you choose to send; do not send media unless needed for your request. Contact adityapatil206999@gmail.com for privacy questions.
